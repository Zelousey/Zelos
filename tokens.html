Questions

What are tokens?

Site credit for AgenticTrading.info. They unlock live scanner alerts (a 7-day pass or one alert) and may unlock other premium tools later. They're separate from Trade War: Trade War money is virtual and can't be bought, sold or turned into tokens.

Do tokens expire? Can I cash them out?

Tokens don't expire while the site offers them. They have no cash value, can't be transferred, sold or exchanged, and aren't refundable except where the law requires. See the Terms, section 07.

Is anything free?

Yes. Every alert is free for everyone once its trade finishes (it hits a target or stop, or expires), so the full track record stays open. The daily scan pages, Trade War, Squads, Communities, the Real Trade Journal and the games are free. You can also earn free tokens: welcome tokens, a daily check-in streak, winning Trade Wars and the Founder Program.

I bought on Gumroad before. What happens?

Thank you! Gumroad is retired and we honor what you bought: email support from the address you used and we'll add tokens for it.

Is this investment advice?

No. Zelos alerts are rule-based, general information, the same for everyone. They're not personalized advice, and Zelos never connects to or trades in any brokerage account.
