Why Zelos exists

Stock market education should be easier to start.

Many virtual trading platforms aren't built for beginners, and some focus on currencies rather than companies. Zelos is being built to make market education and virtual trading more accessible: a place to explore the market, practice trading, learn strategies and get comfortable understanding markets, all without risking your own money.

It isn't meant to be just another simulator. The goal is to bring virtual trading, market information, education, research, community and competition together, for long-term learning rather than quick wins.

The founder

Built by someone who wanted it to exist.

Zelos was founded by Nathan Groccia, a 19-year-old developer and investor from Massachusetts.

After trying virtual trading platforms, Nathan wanted something different: a beginner-friendly platform focused on actual stocks, real market prices and meaningful financial education. Some platforms were built around forex, trading currency pairs, when what he wanted was to practice with equities: companies everyone knows, like Tesla, Nvidia or Amazon, and to talk about them with people following the same stocks.

“I wanted new traders who are still learning to be able to have real conversations with experienced traders who are trading the real market: the same stocks, the same indexes, the same news, and up to date.”

He also wanted more in-depth tutorials on technical indicators, chart patterns, strategies and the fundamentals of the market. And he's always been interested in history: how people in the past carried themselves, approached challenges, and how their ideas apply to modern life and trading. That interest became the Hall of Minds.

Nathan's own experience is mostly long-term investing in positions he considers relatively safer, alongside some shorter-term and day trading. With an interest in investing, history and technology, he began building Zelos around one idea: learning about markets should be more accessible, engaging and approachable.

Today Zelos keeps growing through experimentation, development and feedback from the people who use it.

Nathan isn't a professional trader, financial advisor or market expert, and Zelos isn't a financial advisory or trading service. It's a place to learn, practice and get more comfortable understanding the stock market.
The future of Zelos

Still early, and actively growing.

Zelos is in development. Here's where it's headed. These are plans and possibilities, not promises.

Virtual trading and education Focus now

The priority is helping new people learn the market and practice with virtual money, and keeping Zelos accessible and affordable, ideally free to get started. Especially for younger people and anyone who doesn't have money to risk yet: get comfortable first, then decide.

Better market data Ongoing

  • Faster charts and more responsive live prices
  • More stocks to trade and more historical data
  • Better news and more detailed financial information
  • Expanded research tools and market coverage

A bigger education library Ongoing

Deeper lessons on technical indicators, chart patterns and strategy, more historical market lessons, and in time lessons from experienced traders and creators who already teach their own audiences. Some of that could be unlocked with Zelos tokens.

Community and competition Experimenting

Community posts, squads, leaderboards and Trade War are experiments in making practice more engaging. Trade War is deliberately more game-like than real trading. What stays and grows depends on what people actually enjoy.

Real trading Maybe, long-term

Zelos could one day expand into real trading, but it isn't the focus and isn't available. It would take substantial development, infrastructure, licensing and regulatory work, so treat it as a possible direction, not a plan.

The long-term vision

A complete place to learn about, practice and engage with the stock market: better data, more lessons and a stronger community, built up step by step.
